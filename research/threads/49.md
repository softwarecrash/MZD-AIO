## Eröffnungsbeitrag

First off thanks for AIO Tweaks!

Issue:
When driving with Google Maps on the car screen and listening to Google Play Music:
I experience audio lag when the google maps view changes perspective. E.g. when the maps zooms in close to a turn, or rotates at a roundabout, music that is playing choppy.

Setup:
MZD-AIO-TI v2.8.4 - installed Android Auto on my European Mazda3, on FW 56.00
Using android auto 4.1.590944-release
Android 9 phone with USB connected.

Other: I had to do the 180 degrees GPS fix.
