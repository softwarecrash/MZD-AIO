## Eröffnungsbeitrag

Hi there,
Thank you all for contributing to Android Auto app for Mazda CX series.
I have a Mazda CX-5 2017. I was successful to download the application and load it to my car. However, when I try to open the Android Auto Map, it shows the message "failed to load plugin '/usr/lib/gstreamer-0.10/libmfw_gst_mpeg2dec.so': lib_mpeg2_dec_arm11_elinux.so.2.2.0: cannot open shared object file: no such file or directory." It freezes on it and at the bottom of that page, it shows" W:/home/mazda/headunit/hu_hu_usb-ccp:446 Start: Nothing found, waiting"
The app doesn't work or launch when I connect my phone through UBS port.
I have tried to install the app again and again a few times, but unsuccessful.
I appreciate if you guide me on how to fix this issue. P.S, my phone is Galaxy S9 and the version of the AIO is 2.7.9
