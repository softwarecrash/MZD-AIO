Everyone gets those errors they are just Gstreamer errors.  You can use the multicontroller to scroll that textbox and when you get to the end you will see something like "nothing found waiting to connect" because it is waiting for you to plug your Android phone to the car's USB.  If your phone is already connected then your USB cable is the problem and that issue is fixed by using a better quality USB cable.
