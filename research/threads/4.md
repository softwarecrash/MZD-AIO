### hansaya – 2018-03-13T15:27:30Z

[Originalkommentar](https://github.com/Trevelopment/MZD-AIO/issues/4#issuecomment-372705776) · Aktualisiert: 2018-03-13T15:27:30Z

WIFI and Bluetooth uses the same frequency. Car system most likely going to have a one modem(using the same antenna) with WIFI and Bluetooth built in. Modem rapidly switches its radio between two if it is using both at the same time, but this should not be a problem since manufacture of those radios should handle it. However I haven't seen a inside of the Mazda system yet so I cannot tell whats going on. Other thing to note is, your phone or any other near by 2.4ghz radio could interfere when they disregard Bluetooth channels.

Have you monitor the system usage during shuttering? You could enable the inlay by pressing Music + Back + Pwr/Mute. http://mazda3revolution.com/forums/2014-2017-mazda-3-skyactiv-audio-electronics/54722-accessing-infotainment-diagnostics-menu.html
This might give you an clue is to whats going on.
