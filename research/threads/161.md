## Eröffnungsbeitrag

I bought a car recently with tweaks installed specifically AA Auto and the Speedometer. Firmware was 55.x.

Anyways, since the car had the old 1A USB ports, I took it to a Mazda dealer to get the firmware upgraded and also have that new USB hardware box installed, as well as officially installing the AA application and Carplay on the head stock unit.

He told me after about 30 minutes of trying to upgrade firmware that due to the "Hacks" installed it's not possible to use the standard method dealers use to upgrade firmware, and that he needs to communicate with head office for their recommendation, but it might be impossible for them to solve.

He'll get back to me in a few days, but what do you think? Any of you had this experience and was it resolved?
