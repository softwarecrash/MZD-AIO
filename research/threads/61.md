[Originalkommentar](https://github.com/Trevelopment/MZD-AIO/issues/61#issuecomment-525979305) · Aktualisiert: 2019-08-29T01:17:10Z

OK this is what is happening, [drivelist (npm package)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/drivelist) is returning an object containing a drive that has no path, hence drive.path === undefined.  I found that this happens when you have a drive plugged in but unmounted or something like that or maybe a virtual drive that is unmounted or an unused partition.  For the next AIO I had to rewrite all the USB drive stuff because drivelist changed and now uses promises and I put in a check for undefined paths.

It actually happens right after tweaks.sh is done being written on the next step which is to detect USB drives.  So a workaround for now is to make sure you don't have any drives that are unmounted but still connected or virtual drives that are connected but don't have a path or anything out of the ordinary like that with your detectable drives.
