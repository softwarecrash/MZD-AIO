## Eröffnungsbeitrag

There’s a retrofit kit to enable CarPlay and Android Auto on cars 2014 and newer.

More info: https://9to5mac.com/2018/11/21/mazda-carplay/

The retrofit costs about $450-500 at the dealership or $200 if you buy and install it yourself. The retrofit includes new usb ports, but the retrofit is mostly a firmware update. Unfortunately, the firmware won’t enable CarPlay unless new usb ports are detected.

Is it possible to force enable CarPlay without the usb port install? How could I go about doing this?
