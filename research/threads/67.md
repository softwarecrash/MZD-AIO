## Eröffnungsbeitrag

Head Up Display Feedback on 1.12.

A quick little bit of navigation today gave turn by turn and distance countdown in miles. However, after 0.1mi the display on the head-up reverts to meters (m). The screen display show the same numeric value with yds tacked on.

Using AIO again there is a problem with how roundabouts (those funny British road thingies) are drawn. The head up display image of the roundabout and designated exit is flipped about the vertical axis. That means an effective right turn is shown as a left and vice-versa. It's like roundabouts have gone chiral - (for chemists among you)

I found everything else worked well, Google maps was perfect. Google music played without stuttering. A phone call notification popped up. (no number ID so it was declined so no information on call quality). Although after a break and coming back to the car music playback wouldn't even though play was clicked. I needed to pull and re-fix the USB cable - not sure if that was initially poor cable seating or not.
