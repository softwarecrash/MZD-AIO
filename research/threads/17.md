## Eröffnungsbeitrag

I installed FW 70.00.000 EU from [this source post in the forum](https://mazda3revolution.com/forums/2014-2018-mazda-3-skyactiv-audio-electronics/98202-mzd-connect-all-firmware-version-only-downloads-290.html#post2399283).
I still have the old USB-Hub, not the new (needed one).

The pause on mute tweak did not work properly: Pressing mute once playing a mp3 from a USB stick the time continued, but it was silent. Pressing mute the second time, some strange status occured. Exiting and getting back to normal was only possible via commander knob.

Android Auto from AIO was not possible to start via cable - blackscreen on MZD (needed to reboot) and on the smartphone AA App always restarts in a loop. AA Wifi from AIO was not possible to test, because my test-CMU does have the wifi-bug and can not connect to anything via wifi.

So these two tweaks seem not to be ready for 70.00.000 EU.

Tweaks without a bug in my test:
- Touchscreen while moving
- Speedometer (Digital Bar)
- No More Disclaimer
- Reverse Camera Safety Warning
- Semi-transparent parking sensor graphic
- Order of Audio Source (FW 70 checkbox was ticked), but neither AA nor CP was visible in the list of audio sources at this 70.00.000 EU

A german Mazda dealer told me today that 70.00.012A EU is the first official FW to download at the moment, so 70.00.000 EU will not be released officially.
