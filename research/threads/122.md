Hi @tmaiaroto there are 3 ways to share internet with an android device but they all have their caveats and I don't know them all
1. Connect to phone hotspot with car wifi
2. Start AP from car and connect with phone wifi
3. ADB via USB cable

Android auto uses its own protocol through USB and you can learn more about that in this and other AA project repos  https://github.com/gartnera/headunit
CASDK is pretty sweet because you can make whatever app you can figure out how to make and so I recommend fuxing with it and feel free to ask me if you have any more questions.
