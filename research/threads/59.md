An attempt to kill the video is in the _noLongerDisplayed but that is much too late so it is also attempted to be killed in _StartContextOut and also every 10 milliseconds it checks the context and if the app context is not videoplayer an attempt is made.  So 3 attempts are made so that the video is killed as soon as possible upon exiting the videoplayer app and whichever is successful first will kill the video.
