There are some features that I wish this project could have supported. They are Android Auto features which the stock OEM can do - I can only tell these features because I installed OEM retro-kit on the family car, but not my car:

1) Offer the Android Auto icon in the top left of the Mazda Connect home screen when you connect your phone as a short cut
2) Allow switching between Android Auto/Mazda Connect via 3 second hold of home button (instead of end call button)
3) When you are on any Mazda Connect screen, pressing the NAV button will navigate to google maps (if you have Mazda Navigation, then this shortcut does not apply)
4) If you are playing music from Android Auto but are on a Mazda Connect screen, pressing the MUSIC button will navigate to Android Auto media playing

With that being said, these are obviously features which I wish were implemented, but they are certainly not _needed_. It is completely reasonable to say that if I desire these things that much, I should just buy the upgrade kit and install it - and I may do that in the future. Anyways, this project has definitely served its purpose in providing a reliable means for modification of the Mazda Connect System.

I realize how difficult it is to develop something like this in your free time @Trevelopment, and I thank you for your work on the project and sharing it with the world. This has provided value to my vehicle a lot, and I love using my car even more because of it.
