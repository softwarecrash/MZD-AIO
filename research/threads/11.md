## Eröffnungsbeitrag

Any ideas on having a max volume on radio start? It'd be nice for those times after a highway drive you're at a 32 maybe. Then the next day you start up the car, you're not immediately blasted with a 32. It's really nice on my work vehicles where it's a max of 16 on those, so you don't get blasted by someone leaving it loud on accident.

I was going to poke around at it myself to see how to make this happen, but figured I'd try putting it out there if it's one of those "Oh I can do it in 5 min" jobs someone smarter than me can do.

Hey I took a look and found in /jci/gui/apps/audiosettings/js/audiosettingsApp.js how to set the "audioAutoVolume" volume using JavaScript:
` framework.sendEventToMmui(uiaId, appData, { payload : { audioAutoVolume : level }});`
I'm not sure if this will do exactly what you want but its worth a try.
